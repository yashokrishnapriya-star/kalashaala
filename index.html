Founder

Our founder, Ms. Yasho Krishna Priya.N, is a passionate Bharatanatyam dancer, a state-level yoga silver medalist, a Carnatic vocalist, and a Junior Miss India Season 4 finalist. She is an inspiring young Indian classical dancer and model who has quickly made a name for herself on the national stage.

She possesses an exceptional talent for Bharatanatyam, an ancient and expressive classical dance form from South India that requires rigorous discipline, precise footwork, and vivid facial storytelling. Balancing her traditional arts with contemporary fashion, she successfully ventured into the highly competitive world of youth pageantry.

Her dedication culminated in a remarkable performance at the prestigious Junior Miss India Season 4 national pageant. The grand finale of this mega event took place on January 8, 2026, amidst the royal splendor of the iconic Hotel Clarks Amer in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Competing in the highly contested 13 to 14 years age category, she captivated both judges and audience with her graceful stage presence and immense poise.

Her deep-rooted cultural grounding shone brightly during the specialized Cultural Round, where she outperformed her peers to take home the winner's title. Furthermore, her spectacular dance abilities earned her the coveted national subtitle of Miss Talented, cementing her status as an elite young performer in the country.

This double victory catapulted her into the spotlight, making her a celebrated role model for aspiring young artists across India. Following her pageant success, she continues to expand her horizons by collaborating on professional modeling assignments, portfolio shoots, and cultural showcases.

She maintains an active digital presence through her official Instagram Page, where she generously shares her journey, dance reels, and behind-the-scenes glimpses of her rising career with her growing community of supporters. Through her hard work and artistic excellence, she beautifully demonstrates how modern Indian youth can honor their cultural heritage while simultaneously chasing contemporary dreams.

Her achievements serve as a testament to her family's steadfast support, her teachers' guidance, and her own relentless work ethic. As she continues to grow both as a classical dancer and a model, the future looks incredibly bright for this multi-talented teenager. Ultimately, her story inspires other young children to fearlessly pursue their passions and proudly showcase their traditional roots on global platforms.
